## Who We Are

A small group of builders in Thailand who got tired of opaque air-purifier marketing. We started assembling box-fan filters for our own families, then helped neighbors and schools do the same.

## How We Work

- We publish our part lists and sources so you can buy locally.
- We keep margins small and reinvest in community workshops.
- We favor simple, repairable designs over glossy enclosures.
- We answer questions plainly; no fear-based messaging or medical promises.

## Why We Sell Kits at All

Some people want convenience, others want to learn. Kits keep the barrier low while still showing every component. If you prefer to source parts yourself, the [build guide](/build-guide/) is fully open.

## Our Commitments

- Honest communication about performance and limitations.
- Local sourcing where possible to reduce cost and shipping.
- Respect for community partners—schools, cafés, clinics, and volunteers.
- Ongoing updates to guides as we test new fans, filters, and layouts.
