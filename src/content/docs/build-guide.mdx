## Tools & Materials

- One box fan (40–50 cm) with a flat face
- One MERV 13–16 filter matching the fan size
- Painter’s tape or duct tape
- Scissors or a cutter
- Marker for alignment

## Step-by-Step

1. **Prep the fan.** Unplug it. Wipe the intake side to remove dust and ensure the surface is dry.
2. **Check airflow direction.** Mark the intake side with a small arrow; the filter should face the intake.
3. **Align the filter.** Place the filter against the intake side. Ensure the arrow on the filter points into the fan.
4. **Seal the edges.** Tape along every edge with short overlapping strips. Aim for an airtight seal without covering the fan controls.
5. **Support the filter.** If the filter is thin, add a cardboard frame or straps so it sits flat and does not vibrate.
6. **Test safely.** Turn the fan on low first. Check for rattles and air leaks; add tape where you feel airflow escaping.

## Placement Tips

- Use it in the room you occupy most. Close windows during heavy smoke events for best effect.
- Keep the intake at least 20 cm away from walls to allow airflow.
- If noise bothers you, angle the fan away from you and run it on medium.

## Filter Care

- Inspect monthly during smoke season. Replace when the intake side is visibly dark or airflow drops.
- Avoid washing the filter; it damages the media.
- Retape edges after each replacement to keep seals tight.

## Upgrades & Variations

- Add a simple pre-filter (thin mesh or fabric) to catch larger dust and extend filter life.
- Use two filters in a “V” shape for more surface area and lower noise.
- Stackable wood or cardboard frames can make the unit sturdier and safer around kids.

## Safety Notes

- Always unplug before moving or taping the fan.
- Keep the fan away from water and out of walkways.
- This is for particulate reduction, not for gases or chemicals.
