## Practical Specs

- Typical clean air delivery rate: ~150–250 m³/hr per unit depending on fan speed.
- Noise: Similar to a standing fan on low–medium. We include tips for quieter placement.
- Power: Standard 220V AC box fan. Average use: 40–70W.

## Caring for Your Unit

- Replace the filter every 3–4 months in heavy smoke season, or when the surface darkens.
- Vacuum the pre-filter side gently to extend life.
- Retape any air gaps; good seals make a noticeable difference.
