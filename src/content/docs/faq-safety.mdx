## FAQ

**Does this replace policy or clean outdoor air?**
No. DIY filters reduce indoor PM2.5. We still need clean-air policy and community action.

**Is it safe to run a box fan with a filter?**
Yes when built correctly. The fan works a bit harder, so we use quality fans, keep the filter sealed, and avoid blocking the motor vents.

**How long does a filter last?**
Typically 3–4 months in heavy season, longer in lighter pollution. Check monthly and replace when dark or when airflow drops.

**Will it remove odors or gases?**
No. This is for particulate matter (PM2.5 and larger). Charcoal filters help with odors but add cost and resistance.

**Can I buy parts myself?**
Absolutely. We share part lists and tips. Kits are for convenience, not lock-in.

**How noisy is it?**
Similar to a standing fan. Use medium speed for a balance of clean air and comfort; place the unit a few meters away if needed.

## Safety Notes

- Unplug the fan before taping, cleaning, or moving the unit.
- Keep cables tidy and away from walkways.
- Do not leave the fan running unattended around small children without a guard.
- Avoid moisture and never use near open flames.
- Replace damaged cords or wobbly stands before use.

## Troubleshooting

- **Air leaks around the edges?** Retape with overlapping strips; check corners carefully.
- **Rattle or vibration?** Add a cardboard frame or elastic straps to hold the filter steady.
- **Weak airflow?** Clean the fan grill, check for clogged filters, and confirm the fan spins freely.
- **Dust still visible indoors?** Run the unit longer, close windows, and place it closer to the source of smoke.
