## What This Is (and Isn’t)

- Mechanical air filtration using standard MERV box filters and a box fan.
- Proven DIY design used worldwide and tested for PM2.5 reduction.
- Not a medical device and not a replacement for clean-air policy.
- Built for households, cafés, schools, and workshops that want practical relief.

## Why It Works

- **High airflow:** A box fan moves a lot of air through a large surface area.
- **PM2.5 capture:** MERV-rated filters are designed to catch fine particles.
- **Low cost per clean-air volume:** Simple parts, no proprietary consumables.
- **Repairable and upgradable:** Swap filters, tape gaps, and improve over time.

## Why DIY (Even Though We Sell Kits)

DIY means autonomy and understanding. We show every step, list every part, and welcome you to buy locally if you prefer. The goal is clean air that you can maintain yourself—not a dependency on us.
