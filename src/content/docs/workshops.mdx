## What to Expect

- Small groups (6–12 people) so everyone gets hands-on time.
- We provide tools, parts, and safety notes. You can bring your own fan or filter if you like.
- You leave with a working unit, a printed guide, and the confidence to teach others.

## Format

1. Short intro on PM2.5 and how mechanical filtration works.
2. Guided build with safety checks and airflow testing.
3. Tips on maintenance, placement, and low-noise setups.
4. Q&A plus time to share local sourcing tips.

## Pricing

- Standard seat: covers parts, space, and facilitation.
- Bring-your-own-fan option at a lower cost.
- Discounts for teachers, community volunteers, and group bookings.

## Upcoming Sessions

- Chiang Mai: Monthly during smoke season (Feb–Apr)
- Bangkok: Twice monthly year-round
- Custom sessions: Available for schools and cafés on request

## How to Join

Message us with your city, preferred weekend, and whether you need a full kit. We confirm dates, share the materials list, and keep groups small for a calm, practical build.
